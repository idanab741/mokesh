Questions.
Before we start.

How long does it take to build an app?

From idea to the stores takes up to 8 weeks, including Apple and Google review. The exact timeline depends on the scope of the product.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short discovery call you get a fixed-price proposal for the first version, with a clear list of what’s included.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

Yes. Store listings, screenshots, a privacy policy and replies to the reviewers, all the way to approval on the App Store and Google Play.

Who owns the code and the app?

You do. The app is published under your own developer account, and the full code is handed over to you.
